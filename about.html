About QuickTools Hub

Simple online tools built to save time on everyday calculations.

Our goal

QuickTools Hub is a small independent website focused on practical browser-based tools. We build calculators that are easy to understand and quick to use, especially on mobile devices.

What we provide

Our tools cover common calculations such as percentages, BMI estimates, loan EMI estimates, GST, discounts, unit conversions, word counts and tips.

Accuracy

We test the formulas used by our tools, but results should be treated as general estimates. For important financial, medical or business decisions, verify results with a qualified professional or authoritative source.
